JAMAICAN PASSPORT RENEWALS

Why Keeping Your Jamaican Passport Current Matters — and Why to Plan Ahead

A valid passport is much easier to deal with before you urgently need it. Travel plans, identification needs, family matters, or unexpected circumstances can become more complicated when a passport is close to expiry or already expired.

Starting the renewal process early gives you time to gather required documents, check your information, and deal with questions before a deadline is pressing.

Requirements and processing times can change, and individual circumstances can affect what is needed. Check the current requirements of the appropriate Jamaican authority before making plans that depend on a renewed passport.
