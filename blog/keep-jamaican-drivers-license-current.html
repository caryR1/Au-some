JAMAICAN DRIVER'S LICENSE RENEWALS

Keeping Your Jamaican Driver's License Current While Abroad

When you live outside Jamaica, it can be easy to lose track of the expiry date on a Jamaican driver's license. Checking renewal requirements before the license expires can save time when you next need it.

Planning ahead gives you an opportunity to confirm the current process, gather identification and supporting documents, and resolve questions without the pressure of an immediate deadline.

Renewal rules, fees, and documentation requirements can change. Current information from the applicable Jamaican authority should guide your renewal.

Au-Some Notarific Services LLC can help clients organize documentation for Jamaican driver's license renewal. This is an independent support service and does not issue licenses or represent a Jamaican government authority.
